Berlin International University of Applied Sciences Hakkında
Berlin International University of Applied Sciences, Berlin’in merkezinde bulunan, devlet tarafından tanınan ve kâr amacı gütmeyen bir Alman uygulamalı bilimler üniversitesidir. Üniversite, öğrencilerin akademik, kültürel ve dilsel potansiyellerini geliştiren ve onları küreselleşen bir dünyada uluslararası kariyerlere hazırlayan zorlu, yenilikçi ve uygulamaya yönelik bir eğitim sunmaktadır.

What is the Berlin Semester Programme?
Berlin Semester Program is a student exchange program that allows Bahçeşehir University students to study as exchange students at
Berlin International University of Applied Sciences
for one or two semesters.
About Berlin International University of Applied Sciences
Berlin International University of Applied Sciences is a state-recognised, non-profit German university of applied sciences located in the center of Berlin. The university offers a challenging, innovative and practical education that develops students’ academic, cultural and linguistic potential and prepares them for international careers in a globalizing world.

Application Conditions:
Being a full-time bachelor student registered at Bahçeşehir University,
Being registered in the program departments and having completed the first year,
Having a GPA of at least 2.00,
Having a course load of at least 20 ECTS,
What are the Tuition Fees and Scholarship Opportunities?
No additional fee is requested from the student other than the education payment made to Bahçeşehir University, and the student’s current scholarship is also preserved.
